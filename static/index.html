Video call

Voice, video, chat and files go straight to the other person — never through a server. End-to-end encrypted. No account, nothing stored.

How it works
  • Nothing flows through us. Our server only helps two browsers find each other. After that the call runs directly between you.
  • Encrypted end to end. Nobody in the middle can read or hear anything — not even us.
  • Nothing is stored. No accounts, no recordings, no history. Close the tab and the room is gone.
